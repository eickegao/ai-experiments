# Skill study material (all skills at level 3)

Every skill was cast once on the same real board: seed 1001, after 30 s of play, you are team A (your ball is '@').
'Effect' = cells you gained after the cast minus what you would have gained without casting, measured 1 s after the cast (instant) and 6 s after (lasting); each number is the average of the same cast made at three moments of the game (20, 30, 40 s).
'Aimed' = the ball was first pointed at the enemy mass (FIRE: densest enemy spot; TELEPORT: a pocket enclosed by enemy cells). 'Unaimed' = cast as is.
Boards: '.' neutral, A–D owner, lowercase = that team's ball, '@' = your ball.

## Combos and strategy (our notes)

- **Teleport + Shatter (or Split).** Teleport into a SMALL pocket enclosed by one enemy colour, then cast Shatter (or Split) at once. Inside the pocket the ball flips a cell on almost every bounce; with Shatter every flip spawns another ball (1 → 2 → 4 → …), with Split you have five balls doing it. A whole region of that colour collapses within a few seconds. This is the strongest thing in the game when it lands.
- **Hit the leader, not everyone.** Look at the cell counts: aim teleports, fires, giants and shockwaves at the team that is currently first. You do not need to out-paint the whole board; knocking the leader's biggest block down is usually what makes you first.
- **Shockwave grows with distance.** The arc widens as it travels, so cast it from FAR away, pointed at a big enemy block: the rays spread out and paint a wide front. Cast right next to the enemy the arc is still narrow and paints only a few cells.
- **Giant and Fire are finishers.** Their paint cannot be answered in the last seconds: keep one charge for the final 5 s and put it into the leader's territory.
- **Accel is a multiplier, not a plan.** It only pays when something else is happening: a ball driving into enemy ground, Shatter or Split active, a Giant run.

## FREEZE (freeze)

- Demo, aimed: ball at (11.7, 21.4): instant **+1 cells**, after 6 s **+6 cells**.
- Demo, unaimed: instant **+1**, after 6 s **+8 cells**.
- Our tip: Freeze slows (or at level 3 stops) enemy balls that the wave reaches; it paints nothing itself. It is only worth a slot if you plan to use the frozen seconds to paint inside their area.

- Our tip: Lightning paints four lines from your ball to the edges, up to ~63 cells, no aiming needed. It is worth most when your ball sits in the middle of the board or inside enemy colour, so that the lines cross their cells instead of yours.
